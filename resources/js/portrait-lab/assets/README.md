# Portrait atlas v1

The original sheet remains unchanged. See [collection 02](collection-02.md) for the two additive expansion sheets, saved paths and exact generation prompts.

`portrait-atlas-v1.png` is the original 1086 × 1448 RGBA image generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-19/20 for the user-authorized portrait lab proof of concept. No API/CLI fallback or additional dependency was used. Its source was `/home/chad/.codex/generated_images/01a0bbe7-401d-7203-9d84-01ba8482b625/exec-2a8a2919-e784-4497-b4d2-edef6ed2e657.png`; the original was retained. The workspace copy is bundled by Vite, so runtime has no dependency on that source path or an AI service.

The output did not conform to the requested grid coordinates. `../catalog.js` contains measured source crops and adjusted canonical destinations. Runtime canvas composition applies registration, neck coverage and palette masks; this file itself is unmodified. It is exploratory artwork, not a production-ready comprehensive library.

## Final generation prompt (verbatim)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Production prototype sprite atlas for a modular game portrait compositor, CLEAN DIGITAL PAINTING style: refined semi-realistic digital painted faces, even soft front-left studio light, controlled edges, no canvas grain, believable people. Generate ONE image, a 3-column by 4-row regular grid of TWELVE square cells, on GENUINELY TRANSPARENT alpha background. Tall 3:4 aspect ratio. No labels, text, borders, grid lines, shadows cast outside objects, backgrounds or checkerboard pixels. Each cell is an identical virtual 512x512 canvas. Transparent empty space is essential. Each item confined to its own cell. This is a technical asset sheet, not complete portraits; NEVER attach faces to hair or bodies to heads. Do not show complete people.
ALL THREE FACES MUST be strictly FRONTAL looking directly forward, centered symmetrically at local x256, bald scalp starts y100, head widest at x165..347, eyes on y235, mouth y302, chin y340, ears y222..285, neck extends from chin down to y422 and gently flares to x195..317 at bottom. Include head, ears and neck only, no shoulders, clothing, hair, beard or jewelry. Neck bottom is an even horizontal cutoff, intended to be covered by clothing. Equal head dimensions and landmarks between identities.
ROW 1, three isolated bald face-and-neck bases: LEFT Black woman age about 48, dark brown skin, broad cheekbones, subtle age lines, calm expression, natural eyebrows. CENTER East Asian man age about 60, medium warm skin, expressive aged face, no facial hair. RIGHT man age about 30 with light olive skin, broad nose, thick eyebrows, clean shaven, friendly calm expression. All natural non-glamorous faces. They will have interchangeable hair applied later.
ROW 2, three isolated hair wigs ONLY, no heads no skin no eyes no mannequin. Dark neutral charcoal hair with painted grayscale light and shade so runtime can tint it. Same front facing silhouette and coordinates to fit the face above: crown around x256 y90, scalp perimeter around x165..347 y100..190, forehead opening centered x256 and fully transparent below y190, temples follow x165 and347 to y230, ears unobstructed. LEFT short tight natural curls with a characterful textured silhouette. CENTER elegant short side-parted swept-back hair. RIGHT medium wavy tousled hair with modest volume and small fringe. Hair interiors above forehead opaque, actual opening for face transparent. Keep each wig in upper half of its square cell, all lower areas fully transparent.
ROW 3, three isolated shoulder-and-chest garments ONLY, no head no neck no skin no mannequin. Frontal symmetrical torso, same registration coordinates: collar begins y360, shoulders slope from x185 y370 toward x55 y418 and x457 y418, chest fills x45..467 through bottom y512; bottom edge at cell boundary. Leave all area above collar fully transparent. Garments made in neutral GRAYSCALE mid gray fabric with charcoal shadows and ivory highlights, no colored fabric, realistic subtle folds, matte clean digital painting. Runtime multiplies chosen color over grayscale fabric. LEFT civilian tailored blazer over white collar shirt, open collar, no tie. CENTER modern officer high collar service tunic with restrained shoulder tabs and buttons, no actual political symbols, no tie. RIGHT practical worker jacket, sturdy collar and central zipper. Keep collar openings natural and centered to overlap neck bottom at y390..422.
ROW 4, three tiny accessory overlays on transparent full-size square cells aligned to the same face/torso coordinate system. LEFT thin dark round eyeglasses with two lenses centered x212 and300, y237, bridge on x256, lenses transparent without skin or eyes, frame spans x172..340 and y216..259. CENTER neatly trimmed dark mustache ONLY centered x256 y290, spans x218..294 y280..300, absolutely no face or skin. RIGHT dark gray necktie ONLY with triangular knot at x256 y390 and tie tapering to y510, spans x238..274, absolutely no shirt or skin.
Critical: equal grid cells, all specified local positions respected, transparent backgrounds everywhere around the isolated objects, no object centered vertically if its specified coordinates are near top or bottom. This is meant to layer row2 wigs, row3 clothing and row4 accessories over row1 heads, not to display each item as an independently centered illustration. No retro pixel art, no graphic outlines, no photorealistic photos.
```

The prompt describes the requested result; measured output and actual rendering behavior take precedence. The implemented tint technique uses a luminance ramp instead of simple multiplication, and the implementation uses a 512 × 600 portrait registration.
